About US

The Indian Institute of Materials Management (IIMM), Ayodhya is a distinguished regional center of the nationally recognized IIMM, dedicated to the advancement of materials management, supply chain education, and professional excellence. Established with a commitment to promote best practices in procurement, logistics, inventory control, and supply chain management, IIMM Ayodhya has emerged as a hub for developing skilled professionals who are ready to meet the evolving demands of modern industry. As a branch of the apex body of materials management in India, our institute aligns itself with global standards of education and professional ethics. IIMM Ayodhya offers a range of specialized diploma and certification programs such as the Graduate Diploma in Materials Management (GDMM), Post Graduate Diploma in Supply Chain Management (PGDSCM), and other short-term industry-relevant courses that cater to both students and working professionals. Our curriculum is designed to blend theoretical knowledge with real-world applications, ensuring that learners gain not only academic understanding but also practical insights into the challenges and innovations in supply chain systems. With experienced faculty drawn from industry and academia, regular industrial visits, workshops, and seminars, IIMM Ayodhya provides a rich learning environment where students can sharpen their analytical skills, strategic thinking, and operational capabilities.
